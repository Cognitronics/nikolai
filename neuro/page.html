03 — Live

Watch it think.
Talk to it.

NikolAI lives in a small world of five places — hut, orchard, forest, river and workshop — in real time, with its current brain. Everything it hears comes from its teacher; everything it says, it has learned by itself.

World · real time
–

Talk to it

Ask it something – “Where are you?”, “How are you?”, “What do you have?”, “What do you want?”, “What did you do?” – or say anything. It answers with what it learned from reading and from its teacher. Give one of the teacher’s instructions and you are the teacher: if it follows, it learns.

04 — Reading

It reads stories.
And learns from them.

NikolAI reads what a child hears and reads by about twelve – some 100 million words, all from people: parents talking to toddlers, conversations, children’s books, film subtitles, Simple Wikipedia (the BabyLM corpus). It keeps guessing the next word – nobody tells it what words mean. That reading brain is its hearing and speaking. The question: does it then understand instructions with words its teacher never used?

Words it only knows from books

From a start that doesn’t give the answer away (several things to choose from, or the hut with four ways), 4 steps: with the sentence, without it. The teacher never says these words. Control: the same sentence with a made-up word (“Blick the stone!”) – if that works just as well, it only reacted to the noun.
